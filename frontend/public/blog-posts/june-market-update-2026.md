### 1. Months of Inventory (MOI)

Months of Inventory measures supply-and-demand leverage.
It tells us how many months it would take to completely exhaust all active property listings
if zero new homes hit the market. The calculation is straightforward: Total Active Listings ÷ Total Closed Sales.

### Summary and Moving Forward 

The mid-summer data reveals a highly dynamic, fast-moving market. We are seeing a healthy influx of new inventory being met with robust buyer demand, keeping our local real estate landscape moving at a steady clip.

* **For Buyers:** The narrative has shifted from last summer. With new listings up over 131% year-over-year, you finally have genuine choices on the board again. However, don't mistake more options for a slow market—June’s 47.6% surge in closed sales proves that well-priced homes are being actively absorbed. With inventory tightening back down to 3.5 months, the good ones are still moving quickly. If you find a home that fits your criteria be prepared to move quickly. 
* **For Sellers:** Serious buyers are actively out in the market right now, but the landscape is becoming more competitive for you, too. While demand is high enough to drag inventory down to 3.5 months, the data also shows a 15.4% drop in new listings and a 36.8% slowdown in pending sales month-over-month. You no longer have a monopoly on buyer attention. To capture the current summer buying wave before the seasonal autumn slowdown, accurate, data-driven pricing out of the gate is key.

Klickitat county is proposing new rules for Additional Dwelling Units in order to help address affordable housing and make building ADUs easier.

Here are the three biggest takeaways that will impact property owners:

### 1. No More Conditional Use Permits (CUP)
Currently, ADUs require an Administrative Conditional Use Permit and a pre-submission meeting with planning staff. Key regulations include mandatory owner occupancy, strict,7-day minimum rental rules, and required,recorded covenants. Under the new draft rules, ADUs will no longer require a conditional use permit. This means as long as you meet the building standards, you don't need to jump through the CUP hoop.

### 2. Increased Size Limits
*   **Old Rule:** Maximium ADU size 924 sq ft.
*   **New Rule:** Maximium ADU size 1296 sq ft. 

The county is recognizing that modern ADUs need more utility. The draft ordinance increases the maximum allowable square footage for ADUs, giving homeowners more flexibility for multi-generational living or higher-quality rental spaces.

### 3. The "200-Foot Rule" is Relaxed
*   **Old Rule:** The entire footprint of the ADU had to be within 200 feet of the primary residence.
*   **New Rule:** Only the **nearest point** of the ADU needs to be within 200 feet of the primary residence. 

This change allows you to tuck an ADU further back into the trees or utilize the natural topography of your land much more effectively without violating zoning.

---

### What is next for the proposed rule updates? 
The Planning Commission's final recommendations will be sent to the Klickitat County Board of Commissioners. The Board will review the draft, negotiate any final amendments, and ultimately vote to pass the new rules into law.

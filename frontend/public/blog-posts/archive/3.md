# Home Buying Process:

1. **Get pre-approved for a mortgage.** Talk to a lender and get pre-approved for a mortgage loan. This will allow you to know your price range and be taken more seriously by sellers. During the pre-approval process, the lender will evaluate your financial situation, including your credit score, income, and existing debts, to determine how much you can borrow and at what interest rate.

2. **Talk to Nate!** I will sit down and talk to you about exactly what type of house or property you are looking to buy. After I have a good idea of what you want I will setup some automated tools to search for this houses. I am also in good communication with other realtors in the Gorge and keep my eye out for properties before they come on the market officially. I don't believe in pressuring people to buy anything that they will not be extremely happy with. 

3. **Start viewing homes.** I will show you properties and help you decide if it is the right property to make an offer on. 

4. **Make an offer.** Once we've found the right property, I will help you decide on an offer price and negotiate with the seller's agent on your behalf. This includes drafting the contract, handling counteroffers, and ensuring that contingencies (such as inspections and financing) are in place to protect your interests. I always offer by buyers the most protection possible and negotiate in your best interest. 

5. **Get a home inspection.** I will help you hire a professional home inspector to evaluate the condition of the property once your offer is accepted. The inspection will identify any potential issues or needed repairs, which can be used to renegotiate the offer or request that the seller addresses specific concerns. 


6. **Secure financing.** Return to your lender to finalize your mortgage paperwork and costs like down payment, closing costs, etc. At this stage, you'll lock in your interest rate and provide any additional documentation required by the lender.


7. **Close on the home.** Attend the closing meeting with your agent, the seller, and representatives from the lender and title company. At the closing, you'll sign the final paperwork, transfer funds for the down payment and closing costs, and receive the keys to your new home!

8. **Move in!** After closing, you can begin the process of transferring utilities into your name, updating your mailing address, and moving into your new home. Congratulations on becoming a homeowner!

GORGE REALTY WEBSITE AUDIT AND ACTION PLAN
Audit performed: November 2, 2026
Saved: November 3, 2026
Website: https://gorgerealty.com

BUSINESS OBJECTIVE
Generate qualified seller/listing leads in White Salmon, Hood River, The Dalles,
Parkdale, and Trout Lake. Buyer leads are secondary. Favor sustainable systems
that reduce manual follow-up over a broad redesign or constant content posting.

ASSESSMENT
The site has a credible foundation, but currently behaves more like a buyer-search
tool and agent introduction than a seller lead-generation system. Make three
things obvious: why hire Nate, what he will do for the seller, and what happens
when the homeowner contacts him. Improve the conversion path and inquiry handling
before investing heavily in traffic.

SCOPE AND LIMITS
Inspected the live homepage, contact page, listings, buyer map, White Salmon market
page, an education article, active and sold property pages, consultation destination,
robots.txt, sitemap, and representative invalid URLs. Reviewed frontend and backend
source and researched local competitors. Used fresh Chrome profiles at desktop
1440 x 1000 and mobile viewport 390 x 844. Mobile viewport checks are not physical
phone tests or network/CPU-throttled performance tests.

No forms were submitted, appointments booked, emails sent, private production data
accessed, or application files changed during the audit. No authenticated Analytics,
Search Console, Ads, or Google Business Profile data was available. Current traffic,
rankings, conversion rates, spend, and cost per qualified lead cannot be concluded.

WHAT ALREADY WORKS
- Name, portrait, brokerage affiliation, and local focus provide a personal foundation.
- Active listings and sold transactions can support proof of experience.
- Buyer map and market pages offer real functionality.
- Land-use and zoning articles could differentiate local expertise.
- Live sitemap was recently updated. All 12 sampled entries resolved to active,
  published listing records. This is not a complete inventory validation.
- Calendly consultation profile loaded in Chrome. Appointment availability and
  booking completion were not tested. HTTP-only access returned 403, which did
  not mean the browser experience was broken.

FIVE HIGHEST-VALUE CHANGES
1. Repair and reliably record inquiries: correct phone links, persist requests before
   notifications, and track follow-up status.
2. Create a clear seller offer and /sell landing page, with the homepage leading to it.
3. Show seller-specific proof: two documented case studies and actual marketing work.
4. Fix focused performance and SEO issues: portrait size, bundle delivery, metadata,
   canonical URLs, and missing-page behavior.
5. Build focused local discovery: Business Profile plus strong White Salmon and
   Hood River seller pages, then expand where useful.

JOURNEY AND DROP-OFF POINTS
Search/ad -> site: Shared metadata or unconfirmed fee claim creates wrong expectations.
Fix: Match the landing page to selling, pricing, preparation, or the local market.
Homepage -> services: Seller offer requires inference; buyer tools dominate.
Fix: Visible Sell navigation and one primary CTA.
Interest -> inquiry: Generic message box makes the homeowner compose the request.
Fix: Structured property/timing questions and clear offer.
Inquiry -> response: Depends on email; no durable inquiry record in handler.
Fix: Save inquiry, confirm receipt, retry notification, assign next action.
Response -> consultation: No verified inquiry-to-booking connection.
Fix: Associate actual booking/completion with inquiry.
Consultation -> signed listing: Alert engagement isn't a seller pipeline.
Fix: Track qualification, consultation, proposal, signed listing, and outcome.

TECHNICAL SEO APPROACH
The application serves a common HTML shell and renders content with JavaScript.
Google can render JavaScript; do not interpret the shell as proof of non-indexability.
However, API dependencies, non-crawlable cards, metadata bugs, and status codes
create avoidable risk. Start rendering improvements with homepage, /sell, key local
pages and articles. A framework rewrite is not required.

Canonical policy: One preferred hostname and authoritative listing slug. The www
and non-www homepages both served 200 in checks; legacy lokerrealty.com redirected
to the primary domain. Consolidate duplicates intentionally.

Listings: Preserve useful sold pages where permitted. Truly removed pages need an
intentional status-code and retention policy. MLS descriptions alone do not provide
a durable differentiator; original seller guidance and case studies matter more.
Duplicate MLS descriptions do not automatically imply a duplicate-content penalty.

Structured data: Correct listing availability/address fields; add accurate business
identity and breadcrumbs where appropriate. Do not fabricate an office address or
expect schema alone to improve rankings.

Geographic coverage: Parkdale is not in current location navigation. The tested
/market/parkdale route could not load data (API 404); this was a coverage gap, not
an observed broken menu link. Add target-area pages only with genuinely useful content.

TRAFFIC STRATEGY
1. Referrals: Often high-quality when relevant. Low cash cost, modest relationship
   work, potentially immediate. Give past clients and local professionals a useful
   seller page to share; ask for introductions.
2. Google Business Profile: Strong local intent; low cash cost, setup and light ongoing
   maintenance; weeks/months to evaluate. Verify ownership, accurate name/contact
   details, appropriate category, genuine location/service area, photos and neutral
   review requests. No fake offices or review gating. Adding service areas does not
   eliminate the distance factor in local rankings.
3. Permission-based email: Strongest with known homeowners. One useful monthly
   message, separate seller segmentation from buyer alerts, link to consultation
   or a relevant case study. Do not enroll every inquiry automatically.
4. Focused local SEO: Potentially strong intent; moderate initial effort, months to
   assess. Publish two strong local seller pages before expanding to five.
5. Google Search Ads: Intent can be strong, economics unknown. Direct spend and
   weekly search-term review; early signals in days/weeks. Test only after capture,
   tracking and follow-up are verified.
6. Broad social posting: Variable quality and potentially high time cost. Repurpose
   existing useful work; do not make daily posting the main strategy.

OPTIONAL PAID SEARCH EXPERIMENT -- DO NOT LAUNCH WITHOUT APPROVAL
Proposed ceiling: $300 total over 30 days, after tracking is verified.
Start with tightly focused seller terms for White Salmon and Hood River; use the
relevant seller landing page. Exclude clearly irrelevant rental, employment and
buyer-search queries. Review actual search terms.
Success: Genuine qualified seller inquiries and booked consultations, not clicks
or newsletter subscriptions. Review at $150; pause if there are no genuine seller
inquiries and diagnose queries, landing page and tracking. Stop at $300 unless
an extension is deliberately approved. Small/inconclusive samples do not prove
that the channel cannot work. No search volumes, conversion rates or traffic
forecasts have been assumed.
Use housing-appropriate targeting; Google restricts demographic and ZIP targeting
for housing advertising. Do not launch campaigns or spend money as part of this plan.

MEASUREMENT AND FOLLOW-UP
Funnel: Inquiry received -> Qualified seller -> Consultation booked -> Consultation
completed -> Listing signed.
Qualified seller: Owner/authorized decision-maker with a property in the service
area and plausible selling intention; an email open alone is not qualification.

    time, stage, next action and outcome. Capture click IDs only as appropriate to
    consent setup. Keep names/emails/phones/property addresses out of analytics payloads.
[ ] Fire inquiry conversion only after server accepts and saves it.
[ ] Deduplicate submission IDs and normalized contact matching.
[ ] Classify spam, duplicates, buyers, vendors and tests separately.
[ ] Treat phone clicks and booking-link clicks as intermediate events, not appointments.
[ ] Record actual bookings, completed consultations and signed listings separately.
[ ] Exclude Admin activity and sensitive unsubscribe URLs from public analytics.
[ ] Automate receipt confirmation, notification retries and unhandled-inquiry reminders.
